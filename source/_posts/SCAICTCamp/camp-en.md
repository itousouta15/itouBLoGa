## Foreword

To all the partners who have dedicated themselves to this camp:

Honestly, I'm not the type of person who writes in a literary style. Writing such a long piece all of a sudden feels a bit strange.

But lately, perhaps because I've been reading too many tragic novels, I've become a bit more sentimental.

Somehow, I suddenly wanted to share a few things with all of you.

## Main

### A Word of Thanks

First, I want to say thank you to every single one of my partners.

Thank you for being willing to invest your own time, energy, and even responsibilities that weren't originally yours. Many things could have been left undone, but you did them anyway; many tasks could have been ignored, but you chose to stay and help.

I especially want to thank a few partners.

Thank you for tirelessly handling so much work that wasn't necessarily your responsibility, for pushing the entire team forward in ways I couldn't see. Honestly, when I was most lost, seeing you still putting in effort gave me a glimmer of hope.

### My Lowest Point

To be completely honest, I didn't really know what I was doing during this period either.

Last week was probably the most depressing time since we started preparing.

Registration numbers were slow to grow, and watching other camps' numbers increase continuously made my anxiety stronger and stronger. We had held countless meetings, made countless adjustments, researched various promotional methods, and did everything we could.

But the results never came as expected.

I started to doubt whether I had done something wrong, and whether our direction of effort was correct.

Gradually, I chose to escape.

I knew there were still things to handle, I knew there were still problems waiting to be solved, but I couldn't muster the energy to face them. I buried myself in my own world, buried myself in other things, pretending I couldn't see those numbers for a while.

### The Real Weight

However, what really overwhelmed me wasn't just the registration numbers.

It was when I started thinking about the worst-case scenario, and countless problems kept floating in my mind.

If the event ultimately couldn't happen, I would still have to continue communicating with the venue; I would have to explain to sponsors and handle the aftermath; I would have to face the lecturers, staff members, and all the people who have supported us along the way.

What hurt even more was knowing that this isn't just an event.

It's the result of months of preparation by a group of people—the product of opening meetings repeatedly, revising proposals, designing courses, contacting lecturers, and handling administrative and promotional work.

I was terrified that one day I would have to tell everyone that these efforts wouldn't have a chance to be seen.

I was also terrified that those students who might have had the opportunity to come here would ultimately be unable to experience the joy, growth, and fulfillment we wanted to bring them.

These things kept weighing on my heart, making it hard to breathe.

### Why I Could Stand Back Up

But recently, I've slowly figured something out.

Even when I was running away, even when I was at my lowest, there were still people quietly doing the work.

People were still thinking of new promotional methods; people were earnestly contacting partner organizations; people were repeatedly adjusting course content; people were willing to keep believing this event was worth doing, even when no results were visible.

And that's why I was able to stand back up.

### Until the Very End

Perhaps the current situation still isn't ideal.

Perhaps the next two weeks will still be full of uncertainty.

But there's still time until the end, and we're still standing here.

If we have to fail, it should be after giving our all; if there's still a chance, we should keep fighting until the very last moment.

In the days to come, I will also return to my position, think with you, act with you, and face the challenges ahead together.

Whatever the final outcome, I hope that in the future, when we look back, we can say:

"We really did our best."

Thank you to all of you for your company and dedication along the way.

And thank you for choosing to believe in this camp even when I wavered.

Let's walk this final stretch together.
