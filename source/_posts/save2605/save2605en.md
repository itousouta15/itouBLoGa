I honestly don't even know why I'm writing this.
The more concrete reason is probably that I saw Each write one and thought,
".....this feels great~~ I'll write one too."
It doesn't have to be that every blog post is so heavy, and I can reflect on why I don't write often ：P

It's been really hot lately. If I stay outside, I feel like I'll melt.

## Summer Training
Honestly, this has been the thing giving me the most headache lately. The registration situation makes me a bit worried... I've been thinking about what I can do to get more people to sign up. It's really a troublesome issue...

But recently it seems like I accidentally stressed out the deputy leaders.
I really feel bad about it. If you see this~ I'm saying sorry here, Orz

I'm really sorry I couldn't be a good leader.
I accidentally became the person I hate most.

## Otaku Life
Lately I've gotten into reading light novels again.

It's strange because I'm the busiest I've ever been,
yet I feel even more like escaping into stories.

But it's not entirely bad.
At least it makes up for that most confused, aimless period I had before.

I don't have the energy to go to school,
and the summer training thing has left me no time to take a breath.

So many things are weighing me down,
and I don't really have someone close to talk about all this rubbish with.

Day after day.

School, after-school, eat, sleep.

Day after day.

This kind of life gradually suffocates me, but the big breaks in the past few weeks (TWNOG, school fair) helped me feel much better. After all, there's nothing that a nap can't solve! If it doesn't work, it just means I didn't sleep enough ：)

If you ask what I've been reading lately~
I just finished "Becoming Friends with the Second Cutest Girl in Class" — Ai's real wife :D It made me feel that old sensation of rolling around on the bed and curling up into a ball (x

And I'm currently reading "Imouto Life."
Rather than calling it a light novel, I feel it's more like light literature. Watching the boy and girl slowly understand each other, work through their differences, and find the right distance. Sometimes I lose track of time and accidentally read until dawn. It may be a little unhealthy, but that kind of life is pretty nice too.

To some extent I really admire that kind of life. But if it were me, I'd probably pass. Dreams are most beautiful.

## School
Honestly, I can clearly feel that I've completely given up, and I got the lowest score in history on the second exam... but I was busy with other things! What does it matter.... (No... obviously it matters.)

Maybe because I've been reading too many novels or because I feel bad about slacking off so much, recently I started trying to actually pay attention in class! The last time I seriously attended a full day of classes was probably in first year of high school. But I feel it might just be a three-minute enthusiasm for me, lol

Day after day goes by, and the semester is almost over. So many reports are due. What a pain, but I still don't want to do them. I need to finish my novel first!! (the version of me who piled up a bunch of things the next day :)
